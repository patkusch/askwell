Name: Approve a large refund
Actor: Finance manager
Goal: Decide on a refund request over 500
Trigger: A refund over 500 arrives in the waiting list
Preconditions: The manager is signed in and the order exists
Main flow:
  1. Manager opens the waiting list.
  2. System shows each request with order, amount and reason.
  3. Manager selects a request.
  4. System shows the order history and earlier refunds.
  5. Manager approves the refund.
  6. System records the approver, time and amount.
  7. System emails the customer the decision.
Alternative flows:
  5a. If the manager rejects the refund, the manager gives a reason and the flow continues at step 6.
Exceptions:
  7e. If the email cannot be sent, the system retries and alerts support after three failures.
Postconditions: The refund is decided, recorded and the customer informed.

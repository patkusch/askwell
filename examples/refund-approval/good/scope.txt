Objective: Cut wrongly paid refunds below 5 a month and decisions under 24 hours by December 2026
In scope:
- A waiting list for refunds over 500
- Approval screen that records approver, time and amount
- Decision email to the customer
Out of scope:
- Refunds under 500, which stay automatic
- Changes to the billing system
- Chargebacks from card providers
- Mobile app version, deferred to Q2 2027, owner Head of Finance
Assumptions:
- Finance confirms the 500 limit in writing by 2026-10-30
Constraints:
- Live before the December month-end
Change control: Anyone may request a change; the project lead assesses cost, time and risk; the sponsor approves.

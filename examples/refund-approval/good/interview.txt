Interviewee: Maria Costa, refund agent
Date: 2026-09-18
Goal: Understand how a large refund moves from request to payment today
Findings:
- [heard] Large refunds wait in a shared inbox until a manager has time
- [seen] She copies the approved amount from an email into the payment screen, which takes about 4 minutes each
- [seen] She checks the order number in a second system before every refund
- [inferred] Most wrong payments probably happen at the retyping step
Pain points:
- Retyping amounts takes about 6 hours a week and causes typing errors
- Managers answer by email at different times, so no one knows what is waiting
Workarounds:
- Keeps her own spreadsheet of waiting refunds
Candidate requirements:
- Approved amount reaches the payment step without retyping
- A single list of refunds waiting for approval
Open questions:
- Ask Tom Reyes whether the payment screen can accept an amount from another system

Problem: 42 refunds were paid wrongly in March 2026, costing about 9,000
Why 1: Because the amount paid did not match the amount approved
Why 2: Because agents retype the approved amount into the payment screen
Why 3: Because the approval screen and payment screen share no data
Why 4: Because the two systems were bought separately and never linked
Evidence: Of 42 cases, 38 were typing differences in the amount (March payment file against approvals)
Evidence: Agents confirmed they retype it; screen recording of five cases
Evidence: Architecture diagram shows no link between the two
Root cause: The approval and payment screens share no data, so the amount is retyped
Fix: Pass the approved amount into the payment screen automatically and block payment if it differs
Measure: Wrongly paid refunds below 5 a month by December 2026

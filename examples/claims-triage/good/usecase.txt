Name: Triage a new claim
Actor: Claims system
Goal: Place a new claim in the right urgency band
Trigger: A customer reports a loss
Preconditions: The policy exists and is in force
Main flow:
  1. Customer reports the loss and answers the injury question.
  2. System checks the policy is in force.
  3. System reads the injury answer and the repair estimate.
  4. System applies the triage rules.
  5. System records the band, the rule used and the time.
  6. System texts the customer that the claim was received.
  7. System places the claim in the right handler list.
Alternative flows:
  4a. If injury is reported, the band is urgent and the flow continues at step 5.
Exceptions:
  2e. If the policy is not in force, the system tells the customer and refers the claim to a person.
Postconditions: The claim has a band, a record of why, and the customer has been told.

# Personal data (any field)

**Questions to add**
- What personal data is collected? Is any of it sensitive (health, ethnicity, beliefs, biometrics, children's data)?
- Why is each item needed? If there is no reason, don't collect it.
- What is the legal basis for each use, as decided by the privacy or legal owner?
- Who receives it, including suppliers and countries outside the home region?
- How long is it kept, and what triggers deletion?
- How will someone see, correct, export or delete their data?

**Typical requirements to propose**
- Data minimisation: collect only what the purpose needs.
- Purpose recorded for each field in the data dictionary.
- Retention periods and automatic deletion or anonymisation.
- Access control and an access log.
- Ability to find all data about one person, and to export or delete it.
- Consent capture and withdrawal where consent is the basis.
- Breach detection and a path to report within the legal deadline.
- A privacy impact assessment before launch for high-risk processing.
- Test and training environments without real personal data.

**Traps**
- Logs and backups that keep deleted data.
- Free-text fields that collect sensitive data by accident.
- Analytics tools adding third parties nobody listed.

**Rules to confirm** (names only): GDPR and local data protection law, UK GDPR, state privacy laws such as CCPA/CPRA, sector rules.

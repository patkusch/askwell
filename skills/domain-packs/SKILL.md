# Domain packs

Generic BA method is not enough in a regulated or specialist field. Load the pack that matches, apply its checks on top of the normal skills, and be clear about what you are *not* sure of.

| If the work is about | Read |
|---|---|
| Banking, payments, lending, investment | `references/financial-services.md` |
| Policies, quotes, claims, underwriting | `references/insurance.md` |
| Government, councils, agencies, services to the public | `references/public-sector.md` |
| Patients, clinical systems, health data | `references/healthcare.md` |
| Subscription software, product teams | `references/saas.md` |
| ERP, CRM, replacing a core system | `references/erp-crm.md` |
| Reports, dashboards, data warehouses | `references/data-bi.md` |
| Personal data in any field | `references/privacy.md` |

If it spans two fields, read both.

## How to use a pack

1. Read the pack's **questions to add** and ask them in elicitation.
2. Add its **typical requirements** to your list (as candidates, to be confirmed).
3. Run its **traps** against your draft.
4. Flag its **rules to confirm** for the compliance or legal owner. A pack lists the usual rules by name only. Rules change and differ by country, so always have a qualified person confirm the current requirement. Do not state a legal obligation as settled fact.

## Limit

These packs help you ask better questions. They are not legal, clinical or financial advice.
